Trail comparison

RMNP maintains roughly 355 miles of trail. Elevation gain is the total vertical climb from trailhead to destination, which matters more than distance when you are starting above 9,000 feet.

How these numbers were measured

Distances are round trip from the listed trailhead. Figures come from the National Park Service trail pages; third-party guides often report different totals because they measure from overflow parking.

Conditions and safety

Afternoon thunderstorms build fast above treeline in summer. Plan to be below treeline by noon. The park's own guidance is blunt about it:

As a rule of thumb, try to be back at the trailhead by noon.

— Emerald Lake, National Park Service

Pets are prohibited on all trails, tundra, and meadows inside the park.
